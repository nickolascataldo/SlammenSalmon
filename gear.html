Gear Guide

What to bring

Fall mornings on the Pere Marquette are cold and the weather changes fast. Pack for it.

Mandatory

  • Your own waders. No cleats or studded soles permitted in the boat.
  • You must know how to swim.
  • Signed liability waiver before departure.
  • A valid Michigan fishing license.

Recommended

  • Raincoat or waterproof rain gear.
  • Warm layers: fleece, thermal base layers, moisture-wicking garments.
  • Warm insulated, waterproof gloves for cold morning departures.
  • Polarized sunglasses and a brimmed hat for river glare.

Rods, reels and tackle: we can provide everything you need, but you are welcome and encouraged to bring your own favorites. Lunch is included.
