Where to stay

We push off at 8:00 AM, so most anglers arrive the night before. Baldwin (about 15 minutes east) and Ludington (about 25 minutes west, on Lake Michigan) both have motels, cabins, diners and coffee shops. Grab breakfast and coffee on your way in, because the river doesn't have a drive-through.

Good to Know

Questions people ask

Where do we meet in the morning?

At the Upper Branch Bridge River Access in Branch Township at 8:00 AM. It's a U.S. Forest Service site with a vault toilet. The Forest Service lists a day-use parking fee there, so check the current amount. The map above shows the exact spot and has a button for directions.

What if it rains?

We fish rain or shine, and salmon don't mind a gray, drizzly day. We only reschedule for dangerous lightning or an unsafe, blown-out river. Bring good rain gear and warm layers, and see the gear guide.

What's the bathroom situation?

It's a long day on the water, and that's fine. Both the put-in and take-out Forest Service sites have vault toilets, and we'll make natural stops along the river whenever anyone needs one. Just tell your guide.

Will you clean my fish?

If you keep a bright, clean king, we'll help you get it ready at the ramp. Bring a cooler with ice for the ride home. We encourage catch-and-release to protect the run, but the choice is yours.

Is tipping customary?

Never expected, always appreciated.

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes, every angler needs a valid Michigan fishing license. Short-term all-species licenses are available, and you can buy one on your phone through the Michigan DNR eLicense portal. Please do it before the ramp, since cell service can be spotty.

What about lunch and food allergies?

A full lunch is included. Tell us about any dietary restrictions or food allergies when you book, so we pack something you can eat.

Do I sign a waiver?

Yes. You'll sign a paper liability waiver at the ramp before we push off. Tick the box when you book to show you understand.
