Guided Salmon Tours

One day, one river, one goal: salmon

Every trip is a guided drift boat outing focused exclusively on salmon fishing on the Pere Marquette River.

Tue & ThuFull days, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
$500 / tourLunch included. Pay in person.
2 anglers maxOne must speak fluent English
Branch, MIUpper Branch → Lower Branch access

Season & schedule

  • Season runs from the second Tuesday of September through the third Thursday of October.
  • Tours run Tuesdays and Thursdays only, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
  • Pre-reservations open every year on August 1.
  • Rods, reels and tackle can be provided. You're welcome (and encouraged) to bring your own.

Guest requirements

  • Bring your own waders. No cleats or studded soles allowed in the boat.
  • You must know how to swim.
  • A signed liability waiver is required before departure.
  • A valid Michigan fishing license for every angler.

Meet Your Guide

The one at the oars

Your tour is led personally by your guide, who rows the boat, reads the water and puts you on the salmon. Every trip is a small, two-angler outing so you get real hands-on attention from start to finish.

  • Knows the Pere Marquette from Upper Branch to Lower Branch.
  • Handles the drift boat, the net and the lunch.
  • Rods, reels and tackle available if you don't bring your own.
